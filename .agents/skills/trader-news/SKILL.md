# trader-news: NSE India Market News & Announcements Monitor

Tracks breaking financial news, corporate announcements, and regulatory disclosures impacting NSE equities.

## Key Sources & Disclosures
- **Official NSE Corporate Announcements**: Board meetings, dividend declarations, Capex plans, quarterly filings, and bulk deal disclosures.
- **Financial News Outlets**: Moneycontrol, Economic Times, Livemint, CNBC-TV18, Reuters India.
- **Macro Catalysts**: RBI Monetary Policy (MPC rate decisions), CPI inflation data, IIP industrial output, and global market cues (Gift Nifty, US markets, Crude oil prices).
- **High-Impact Event Tagging**:
  - `EARNINGS`: Quarterly result announcements.
  - `DEAL`: Mergers, acquisitions, large order wins.
  - `REGULATORY`: SEBI actions, tax demands, compliance notices.
  - `MANAGEMENT`: Key managerial personnel (MD/CEO/CFO) appointments or resignations.
