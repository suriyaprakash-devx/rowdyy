# trader-analyst: NSE Quarterly Results & Financial Disclosures Analyst

Analyzes official corporate financial disclosures submitted to NSE and BSE under SEBI Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements (LODR).

## Analysis Metrics
1. **Quarterly Operating Performance**:
   - Revenue from Operations (YoY and QoQ growth %).
   - Operating Profit & EBITDA Margin trend.
   - Net Profit (PAT) and Diluted EPS.
2. **Balance Sheet & Solvency**:
   - Debt-to-Equity ratio and interest coverage.
   - Working capital cycle and operating cash flows.
3. **Institutional Read-Through**:
   - Validates whether an intraday breakout has strong fundamental backing or is purely speculative.
