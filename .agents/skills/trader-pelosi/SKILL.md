# trader-pelosi: NSE Bulk/Block Deals & SEBI Insider Tracker

Tracks institutional smart money movements and insider transactions across the Indian capital markets.

## Data Points Monitored
1. **SEBI Prohibition of Insider Trading (PIT)**:
   - Disclosures of promoter, director, and senior executive open market buying or selling.
   - Promoter pledge increases or revocations.
2. **NSE Bulk Deals & Block Deals**:
   - Transactions exceeding 0.5% of total equity shares or ₹10+ Crore institutional block windows.
   - Identifies sovereign wealth funds, major mutual funds, and marquee DII/FII buying.
3. **Daily FII / DII Institutional Net Flows**:
   - Net Cash market buying/selling by Foreign and Domestic Institutional Investors.
   - F&O Index Futures & Options participant-wise open interest (FII Long/Short ratio).
